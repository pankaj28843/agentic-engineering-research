# Future of Software Engineering 2026

This packet turns Thoughtworks' European *Future of Software Engineering 2026*
retreat report into a source-audited reader, practical field guide, podcast
series, and book source. It is written for practitioners who could not be in
the room but want to examine the ideas more deeply than a conference summary
allows.

The report is the agenda, not the proof. Its 53 literal bullet points become 53
independently addressable chapters. Six Part 3 headings become clearly labeled
management interludes without consuming bullet numbers. Every quantitative,
causal, and generalizing claim is checked against extracted source content;
anonymous retreat recollections remain anonymous and are not upgraded by
repetition.

Start with the [reader's guide](guide/00-README.md). It offers three routes:
orientation, evidence audit, and implementation. The
[briefing](briefing.md) gives the compact verdict before the full book.

## Scope and structure

The 53 numbered chapters preserve the report's exact structure:

- 01–05: headline claims and reader orientation;
- 06–35: evidence audits across verification, harnesses, teams, learning,
  modernization, expectations, governance, economics, open source, and human
  judgment;
- 36–53: bounded implementation playbooks with prerequisites, measures,
  exceptions, stop conditions, and rollback;
- six `NNa-...` files: Part 3 management interludes, explicitly not report
  bullets.

The source report is [Thoughtworks, *The Future of Software Engineering 2026:
Europe*](https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/report/tw_future_of_software_engineering_europe_2026.pdf).
[Martin Fowler's index](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/FutureOfSoftwareDevelopment.html)
and linked participant accounts provide provenance and context. They form a
dependent witness network around the retreat, not independent replications.

## Evidence policy

Search-result snippets were used only to find candidates. Every admissible web
claim comes from content read through the repository's headed-CDP workflow or
from a browser-acquired PDF converted with native text extraction. No OCR,
direct-HTTP article fallback, or headless evidence read was used.

Sources keep their class: an official policy owns that policy; a vendor report
owns its measurement and conflict; a preprint owns a provisional study; a
community thread supplies attributed hypotheses; a perspective supplies a
framework, not an effect estimate. “No supporting evidence found” is retained
as a result when a strong public claim cannot be traced.
